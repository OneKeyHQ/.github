## Why OneKey

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>Your keys stay with you</h4>
      We never store your private keys or recovery phrase. They are created on your device and stay there.
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>Everything is open source</h4>
      The app and the firmware are both public. You don't have to take our word for it. Read the code.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>Simple for beginners</h4>
      Use the app on its own, or pair it with a hardware wallet when you want more protection.
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>Encrypted on your device</h4>
      Your data is stored locally and encrypted. Only you can unlock it.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## Where to start

**App**

| Repo | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [app-monorepo](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/app-monorepo) | The OneKey app for iOS, Android, desktop and browser extension |
| [app-modules](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/app-modules) | Native modules used by the app |
| [e2ee-server](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/e2ee-server) | End-to-end encrypted relay server |

**Hardware**

| Repo | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [firmware-pro](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/firmware-pro) | Firmware for OneKey Pro |
| [firmware-classic1s](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/firmware-classic1s) | Firmware for OneKey Classic 1S |
| [firmware-x](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/firmware-x) | Next-generation hardware wallet firmware |
| [firmware-updater-web](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/firmware-updater-web) | Update your device firmware from the browser |

**For developers**

| Repo | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [hardware-js-sdk](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/hardware-js-sdk) | JavaScript SDK for connecting to OneKey hardware |
| [cross-inpage-provider](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/cross-inpage-provider) | Lets dApps talk to the OneKey wallet across chains |

Full guides are at [developer.onekey.so](https://developer.onekey.so).

## Get involved

- **Found a bug or have an idea?** Open an issue in the repo it belongs to.
- **Want to contribute code?** Pull requests are welcome. Start with [app-monorepo](https://github.com/OneKeyHQ/app-monorepo).
- **Need help with your wallet?** Go to [help.onekey.so](https://help.onekey.so). Our team will never ask for your recovery phrase.
